Delete Your Astronomy Logbook Account

For the Astronomy Logbook app by Vedala Holdings

How to request account deletion

Option 1: In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Astronomy Logbook and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings page.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete in the dialog.

Option 2: Without access to the app

Open an issue at github.com/kvedala/astro-logbook/issues titled "Account deletion request". Do not post personal details publicly; state only that you are requesting deletion and we will arrange a private way to verify the account e-mail address.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (e-mail address and login credentials) in Firebase Authentication.
  • Observations, notes, equipment, checklists and other content stored in your account.

What is kept

  • A minimal deletion record (account name/e-mail, account creation and last sign-in dates, sign-in provider, and deletion time) retained to document that the request was honored and for abuse prevention.
  • Anonymous, aggregated diagnostics (crash reports and analytics), which are not linked to your identity.
  • Backups, if any, are purged within 30 days.
