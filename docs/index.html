About

Astronomy Logbook is a free, open-source app for stargazers and astronomers, made by a stargazer. Record what you saw, where and when you saw it, and with which equipment, then search and review your history later.

Features

  • Dark mode to help preserve your night vision
  • Observation log with notes, location and date/time
  • Equipment gallery: add, edit and remove your telescopes, eyepieces and cameras
  • Checklists with swipe-to-delete items
  • Searchable observation gallery with filters
  • PDF export of observations
  • Messier and NGC object catalogs
  • Data stored in the cloud and available on all your devices
  • Only an e-mail address is required to sign in

Usage

  1. Open the app (Android or iOS) and sign in with your e-mail address.
  2. Add your equipment in the Equipment tab.
  3. Create a new observation: choose the object, add notes, equipment, and location (optionally use your device location).
  4. Use the checklist tab to plan targets for an upcoming session.
  5. Browse and filter past observations in the gallery; export them to PDF when needed.
