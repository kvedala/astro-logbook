About

Astronomy Logbook is a free, open-source app for stargazers and astronomers, made by a stargazer. Record what you saw, where and when you saw it, and with which equipment, then search and review your history later.

Features

  • Dark mode to help preserve your night vision
  • Observation log with notes, location and date/time
  • Equipment gallery: add, edit and remove your telescopes, eyepieces and cameras
  • Checklists with swipe-to-delete items
  • Searchable observation gallery with filters
  • PDF export of observations
  • Messier and NGC object catalogs
  • Data stored in the cloud and available on all your devices
  • Only an e-mail address is required to sign in

How to use

The app menu uses these icons: nights_stay Visible tonight comment Observations travel_explore Equipment library_add_check Checklist list List of objects wb_sunny Weather settings Settings.

  1. Open the app and sign in. Open the navigation menu to choose a page from the icon list above.
  2. Before logging an observation, open travel_explore Equipment and tap add. Enter your telescope, aperture, focal length and mount; equipment is selected on each observation.
  3. To record a session, open comment Observations and tap add. Enter a title, optional Messier or NGC number, date and time, observing location and equipment. The app can fill in your location from GPS; you can edit the coordinates and location, or enter them yourself. Add seeing and transparency ratings or notes if useful, then submit.
  4. To review your log, tap an observation to open its details. Tap search to filter by Messier number, NGC number or date range. Long-press observations to select them, then tap picture_as_pdf to export the selection.
  5. Use nights_stay Visible tonight to check target visibility and observing windows for your location. On an object row, open_in_new opens Wikipedia, visibility shows visibility details, the observation icon (add_circle_outline or check_circle) opens matching observations or starts a prefilled log, and playlist_add adds a target to your checklist.
  6. In library_add_check Checklist, tap add for your own reminders, check items off, and tap save_alt to save changes. Swipe an item to delete it.
