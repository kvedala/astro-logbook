Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Overview

Astronomy Logbook ("the app") is a free, open-source app. We collect as little personal information as possible.

Information we collect

  • E-mail address – used only to create your account and sign you in.
  • Observation data you enter – observations, notes, equipment, checklists and related details, stored in your account in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore).
  • Location – only if you grant permission, the app uses your device location to fill in the location of an observation. Location is not tracked in the background.
  • Diagnostics – anonymous usage analytics and crash reports (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) used to improve stability.

How we use information

Your information is used solely to provide the app's features, sync your data across devices, and fix bugs. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check). Google processes data under its own Privacy Policy. Place-name lookups for locations may use your platform's geocoding service.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it or your account. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us using the details below.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in Google Cloud. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/kvedala/astro-logbook.
